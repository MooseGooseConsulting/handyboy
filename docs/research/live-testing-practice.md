# How practitioners test MV3 agent extensions on a live browser (2025-2026)

Researched 2026-10-11 via Exa only. "Read" means I read the source, not just docs.

## Answer first

Almost nobody tests in the user's real, running Chrome. The mainstream: (1) throwaway-profile Chromium or Chrome for Testing (CfT); (2) "real browser" tools attach CDP behind a consent prompt, or via a `chrome.debugger` relay extension; (3) agent quality via repeated live-site evals with an LLM judge. Our native-messaging socket is the rarer, stealthier variant. Correction to our research doc: claim #8 (no CDP attach to a normal Chrome) has been stale since Chrome 144.

## Ranked approaches

**1. Keep: native-messaging channel + streamed JSONL (current live tier).** No CDP, banner or listener; real profile; Wayland-neutral; already built. Peers with a similar extension-bridge shape: [Chrome Relay](https://chrome-relay.kushalsm.com/), [chrome-cdp-extension-bridge](https://github.com/pirate/chrome-cdp-extension-bridge) (unread, adoption unknown).

**2. Throwaway profile on Chromium/CfT (the official path).** Playwright `launchPersistentContext` + `channel:'chromium'` ([docs](https://playwright.dev/docs/chrome-extensions), 97k stars; its [spec](https://github.com/microsoft/playwright/blob/d3d436d3/tests/library/chromium/extensions.spec.ts), read, skips branded channels); Puppeteer `enableExtensions` ([guide](https://pptr.dev/guides/chrome-extensions), 95.7k); Google's [E2E](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/extensions/how-to/test/end-to-end-testing) and [SW-termination](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/extensions/how-to/test/test-serviceworker-termination-with-puppeteer) guides. Google's [PSA](https://groups.google.com/a/chromium.org/g/chromium-extensions/c/1-g8EFx2BBY) for branded Chrome: use CfT/Chromium, or CDP `Extensions.loadUnpacked`, which needed `--remote-debugging-pipe` plus `--enable-unsafe-extension-debugging` (read: [@wxt-dev/runner](https://github.com/wxt-dev/wxt/blob/c2fb8c78/packages/runner/src/install.ts), [chrome-launcher](https://github.com/GoogleChrome/chrome-launcher/blob/main/test/load-extension-test.ts)). A [Chromium CL](https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/+/dddf05b0e69f88da95c3d6d4698521b775f20c2a) dropped both requirements; Puppeteer [#15059](https://github.com/puppeteer/puppeteer/pull/15059) followed (m149). Fit: no real logins, no stealth value, cheap, deterministic. WXT (10.6k) ships only a [Playwright example](https://github.com/wxt-dev/examples/tree/main/examples/playwright-e2e-testing); nanobrowser (13.5k) has Vitest plus the boilerplate's WebdriverIO e2e ([CLAUDE.md](https://github.com/nanobrowser/nanobrowser/blob/master/CLAUDE.md); specs unread), which [broke](https://github.com/Jonghakseo/chrome-extension-boilerplate-react-vite/issues/1013) on branded 137+ until switched to Chromium.

**3. Consent-based attach to the live profile (Chrome 144+).** Enable `chrome://inspect/#remote-debugging`; each client connection triggers "Allow remote debugging?" ([docs](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/devtools/agents/use-cases/auto-connect)). [chrome-devtools-mcp](https://github.com/ChromeDevTools/chrome-devtools-mcp) (53k; read its extension tools and tests) and the [browser-use CLI](https://docs.browser-use.com/open-source/browser-use-cli) (117k) default to it. Its [troubleshooting skill](https://github.com/ChromeDevTools/chrome-devtools-mcp/blob/main/skills/troubleshooting/SKILL.md) says extension tools over an attach need Chrome 149+; CDP-installed extensions vanish on restart (CL above). Cost: a click per connection, [stacking dialogs](https://github.com/ChromeDevTools/chrome-devtools-mcp/issues/1794), and Google's [blog](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/chrome-devtools-mcp-debug-your-browser-session) says the "controlled by automated test software" banner shows, which breaks R-02 for that run. Unverified on our default profile.

**4. Cloned profile, real binary.** Since 136 the debug port is ignored on the default dir ([blog](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/remote-debugging-port)), so everyone copies ([browser-use #1520](https://github.com/browser-use/browser-use/issues/1520)). Launch the same branded binary: Linux keyring names differ, "Chrome Safe Storage" vs "Chromium Safe Storage" ([source](https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/+/ff58622d23a1de5/components/os_crypt/key_storage_linux.cc), read), so CfT silently drops v11 cookies ([macOS twin](https://github.com/vercel-labs/agent-browser/issues/1502)). Copy `Local State`; snapshot Cookies with sqlite `.backup` ([write-up](https://dev.to/zoetaka38/persisting-your-real-chrome-login-across-playwright-restarts-on-macos-126a)). DBSC skips Linux ([report](https://pbxscience.com/chromes-dbsc-stops-cookie-theft-but-linux-is-left-behind/)) but sites may reject transplanted sessions ([oracle](https://github.com/steipete/oracle/pull/258)). Stealth: no. `navigator.webdriver` is set under pipe, not a non-zero port ([web-ext #3388](https://github.com/mozilla/web-ext/issues/3388)), and CDP residue persists though the `Runtime.enable` console tell died in M137 ([Castle](https://securityboulevard.com/2025/08/why-a-classic-cdp-bot-detection-signal-suddenly-stopped-working-and-nobody-noticed/)). The agent sees every cookie; mind site ToS; never enter credentials. Last resort.

**5. `chrome.debugger` relays** ([Playwright MCP Bridge](https://github.com/microsoft/playwright-mcp/blob/eed21856dcf0defa23394909e27125311fed246f/packages/extension/README.md), 37.9k; Claude-in-Chrome). Infobar, contention; keep rejected. Corroboration: a [PR log](https://github.com/microsoft/playwright-mcp/pull/710) shows its relay opening a `chrome-extension://` URL to wake the extension, matching our finding.

**6. Hyprland `sendshortcut`:** reportedly reaches native-Wayland Chromium only when focused ([#6576](https://github.com/hyprwm/Hyprland/issues/6576), [#10351](https://github.com/hyprwm/Hyprland/discussions/10351)); the workaround steals focus. Fallback only.

## Verdicts and fixtures

- Deterministic: local fixture sites ([benchmark repo](https://github.com/ArasHuseyin/browser-agent-benchmark) self-hosts them, flags live drift). [`routeFromHAR`](https://playwright.dev/docs/mock) matches URL, method and POST body strictly: fine for tier 2, impossible without a driver in tier 1.
- Live: Stagehand [evals](https://docs.stagehand.dev/v2/configuration/evals) use 3 trials and an LLM judge over screenshots plus reasoning ([task](https://github.com/browserbase/stagehand/blob/3dddade2/packages/evals/tasks/agent/onlineMind2Web.ts), read). WebJudge reaches ~85% human agreement with o4-mini and warns the agent's own final text hallucinates ([paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.01382)); do not extrapolate to a free Nemotron judge. A toast or pre-reload screenshot is not evidence; check durable state after reload ([ego](https://lite.ego.app/article/browser-use-vs-stagehand-vs-ego)).

## Recommendation

1. Keep tier 1 for live acceptance; only it preserves R-02. Treat CDP-attached runs (3, 4) as functional, never stealth evidence.
2. Correct claim #8 in our doc; record the consent path and banner.
3. When `nb-reload` exits 2, open `chrome-extension://<id>/<self-closing page>` via `google-chrome-stable`.
4. 30-minute spike (user clicks Allow): `chrome-devtools-mcp --autoConnect --categoryExtensions` on 154. Pass = `list_extensions` shows our id and `reload_extension` works. Then it may replace the manual Load-unpacked click and give a post-run observer with less contention than a second extension (inference).
5. Deterministic tier: Playwright fixture (chromium channel), local fixture sites, plus a service-worker-eviction test (`ServiceWorker.stopWorker`), since runs depend on `connectNative` keeping the worker alive.
6. Verdict: structural JSONL assertions plus a read-only post-run extraction compared with the agent's answer. No LLM judge until measured on the free pair.
7. Skip profile cloning unless a task needs logins nothing else gives; check cookie prefix (v10/v11) first.

Unverified: step 4 on 154; cloned profiles keeping the unpacked registration; `sendshortcut` on our Hyprland; DBSC after April 2026.
